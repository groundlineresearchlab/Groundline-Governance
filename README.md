## Purpose

`Groundline-Governance` is the public governance repository for Groundline Research Lab.

It defines the visible governance structure, authority boundaries, repository relationships, research integrity principles, and public accountability framework used across Groundline Research Lab.

This repository does not contain the internal Control Room, private operating instructions, restricted research processes, credentials, security information, or confidential operational material.

## Groundline Research Lab

Groundline Research Lab is the evidence and research layer within the wider MaMeeFarm™ ecosystem.

Its core identity is:

**Data • Evidence • Provenance • Research Systems**

## Core Governance Principles

Groundline Research Lab follows several standing principles:

**Evidence before interpretation.**

**Provenance before reuse.**

**Observation before conclusion.**

**Traceability before simplification.**

**Qualification before certainty.**

Research conclusions must remain within the scope supported by available evidence.

## Repository Independence

Groundline Research Lab may operate through multiple repositories serving different functions.

## Research Integrity

Groundline maintains clear distinctions among:

**Observation:** What was directly found, measured, recorded, or reported.

**Evidence:** Material supporting or constraining an observation.

**Structure:** Relationships, classifications, processes, or system organization identified from evidence.

**Interpretation:** Assessment of what the evidence may mean.

**Hypothesis:** A proposition requiring further evidence.

**Conclusion:** A finding supported within a defined evidentiary scope.

These states should not be treated as interchangeable.

## Provenance

Research material should preserve sufficient provenance to understand:

- Where information originated
- When it was retrieved or observed
- Which record used it
- Whether it is primary, secondary, derived, or interpreted
- Whether transformations were applied
- Whether later records corrected, expanded, or superseded earlier work

Provenance supports verification.

It does not automatically establish factual correctness.

## Historical Integrity

Groundline preserves repository and research history.

Material changes should be documented forward through traceable commits or records.

Earlier states should not be silently rewritten merely because governance, evidence, interpretation, or system structure later changes.

Corrections, revisions, and superseding records should remain identifiable.
